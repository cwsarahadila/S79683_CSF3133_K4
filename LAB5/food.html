About the Festival

Taste of Terengganu Food Festival is an exciting event that celebrates the traditional food and culture of Terengganu. Visitors can explore a variety of local dishes and enjoy the unique flavours of Malaysian cuisine.

The festival brings together local food vendors and visitors in one place. It is a great opportunity to experience traditional Terengganu food, support local businesses and spend quality time with family and friends.
